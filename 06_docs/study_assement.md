In this course, you will be assessed in two ways: a course assignment with a self-review quiz and a final graded quiz.

Course assignment
Throughout this course, you have encountered activities, knowledge checks, in-video questions, and other assessments. Nothing in the final course activity will be outside the scope of what you have already learned, so you should be well-placed to succeed.

This activity will require you to complete a Financial Analysis report for a fictitious project using the data provided. In this Financial Analysis report, you will complete the following:

The revised Estimate at Completion (EAC) and Estimate to Complete (ETC).

The Expected Monetary Value (EMV).

PERT analysis (Three-point estimate).

The total revenue and savings the project would generate over a certain period.

The Net Profit and Return on Investment (ROI) for this project.

A Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA).

Through completing this activity, you will demonstrate your ability to integrate and apply PMP formulas in a practical scenario.

Final assessment
The primary purpose of the assessment is to check your knowledge and familiarize yourself with the types of questions you are likely to encounter at the end of the program. Most importantly, this assessment will help you establish which topics you have mastered and which topics require further focus.

Taking the final assessment
Throughout this course, you have tested your knowledge in practice exams mapped to align with the Project Management Institute (PMI) PMBOK® Guide – Seventh Edition Fundamentals, ensuring you’re well prepared for certification success. While you prepare for the final assessment in this course, you are also preparing for the final exam at the end of this professional certificate by taking practice tests with similar formats and content. You will work towards the final mock exam that concludes the final course.

This assessment contains 30 questions and will take about 1 hour and 30 minutes to complete. You need to score 80% to pass. If you do not achieve this score, you can take the quiz more than once, but note that the questions will vary each time.

The objective of this exercise is to create a financial report for a hypothetical project. You will be provided with the outline and tasked with completing it based on the given case study.

This exercise is designed to enhance your understanding of project evaluation. Take your time to carefully consider each section of the financial report to ensure that your responses align.

Case Study
Assume you are a project manager working alongside members in a large bank's project management office. You and your team have been tasked with developing a mobile banking application that allows users to perform banking transactions, view account balances, transfer money, pay bills, enhance customer convenience, and reduce operational costs.

Project Overview
Project Name: Development of a Mobile Banking Application

Objective: To develop a mobile banking application that allows users to perform banking transactions, view account balances, transfer money, pay bills, enhance customer convenience, and reduce operational costs.

Expected ROI: 25% within two years.

Initial Investment: $500,000

Project Duration: 12 months

Estimated Revenue: $200,000 per year

Estimated Savings: $50,000 per year in operational costs

Project Baselines

Scope Baseline:

Fully functional mobile banking app with core banking features.

Integration with existing banking systems.

Schedule Baseline:

Planning Phase: 1 month

Development Phase: 6 months

Testing Phase: 3 months

Deployment Phase: 2 months

Cost Baseline:

Planning: $50,000

Development: $300,000

Testing: $100,000

Deployment: $50,000

Important project notes
Six months into the project, the team realized that additional security features were needed due to new regulatory requirements. Based on the team's input, you arrived at a revised estimate that the development phase will be extended by two months, and the budget will be increased by $100,000.

To determine the absolute necessity of this extra scope, you seek expert judgment to conduct an impact analysis. The experts warn that if this feature is not included in the mobile application, it will invite a severe threat, with a probability of 90%. If it occurs, it may incur a $80,000 impact on the business, whereas investing in the new feature would mitigate the risk to a 5% occurrence and an impact of $6,000 when it occurs.

You decided to include the new security feature and test the accuracy of the revised schedule using three-point estimation, as your sponsor is keen on finishing the project on schedule. The experts provide the following estimates.

Optimistic = 7 months, Pessimistic = 9 months, and Most Likely = 8 months.

Objective: Financial Reporting
As part of this project, you need to create a comprehensive financial report by completing the following steps:

Calculate the revised Estimate at Completion (EAC) and Estimate to Complete (ETC) due to the addition of scope.

Calculate the Expected Monetary Value (EMV) based on which you decided to include the new scope.

Check for the accuracy of the revised schedule estimate by applying the PERT analysis (Three-point estimate).

Calculate the total revenue and savings the project would generate over 2 years.

Calculate the Net Profit and Return on Investment (ROI) for this project.

Conduct a cost-benefit analysis (CBA) to determine if such projects can be invested in the future and provide your inference.

Include the summary of results from the financial analysis performed.

Include your suggestions and recommendations on whether investing in such projects in the future is advisable.

Review the next sections for further step-by-step instructions.
